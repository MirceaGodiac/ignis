Receipts and qualifications: [ASTRA_RESULTS.md](ASTRA_RESULTS.md).

The statements the models rest on. Each has its evidence, the author's confidence, and the cheapest
test that could break it. Confidence: **high** (rechecked on train by the author, several
sessions), **medium** (supported, but thin, or checked by one reader), **low** (a judgment, one
run, or untested). A claim marked ▲ is one I would attack first.

A finding against a claim goes into [review/](review/README.md) with the claim's id.

**Astra review qualifications, 14 November 2026.** The historical rows below retain their

Brier/squared loss and equal CDF mixtures under CRPS, but it guarantees improvement only
over the mean member score on the same cases, not over the best member. "Different in

not equivalence or non-inferiority. F-006 treats C-33 as an adaptive heuristic, not a
