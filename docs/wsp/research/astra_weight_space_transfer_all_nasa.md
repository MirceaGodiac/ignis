## 1. What the primary literature actually distinguishes

**S.** Takehi, Teoh, Zhang and Khattab's [Sparks of Weight-Space Programming](https://rikiyat.github.io/blog/weight-space-programming/), dated 2 November 2026, describes several different protocols: reading trained weights, editing a trained network, writing a small network directly from rules, and tool-assisted writing with optional forward feedback. Its from-scratch game example excludes data, gradients, tools and prior rollouts. Its larger construction permits Python weight writing and separates feedback budgets. These are reported early experiments in an author publication, not a general theorem of physical transfer.

**D.** “No gradients” is therefore not a complete provenance claim. It does not tell us whether the starting network was pretrained, whether outcomes were inspected, whether numerical arrays were generated by code, or whether candidates were selected by repeated feedback. Each matters independently in IGNIS.

**S.** [Hu et al., arXiv:2611.09382v1](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2611.09382v1), §2 Methods, explicitly studies edits to pretrained Qwen checkpoints, with a Python editing environment, forward observations, candidate selection/reversion and a subsequent held-out evaluation. The reading establishes a feedback-guided editing protocol, not a literal zero-shot physical model construction. Its reported transfer is an empirical question rather than an automatic consequence of sparse editing.

**S.** [Lindner et al., Tracr, arXiv:2301.05062v1](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2301.05062v1), §3, translates a restricted program into transformer components and then weights, using known intermediate representations. It constrains the language/encodings and the finite input vocabulary/context. The introduction separately describes gradient-based compression. Neither compiler-generated arrays nor compressed learned weights automatically satisfy IGNIS's literal author-entry rule.

**S.** [Li et al., Fourier Neural Operator, arXiv:2010.08895v3](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2010.08895v3), §§2–4 and the opening experimental protocol, learns an operator from input/output pairs using parameter optimization. Reusing one learned operator across discretizations is distinct from constructing its weights without training. Its “zero-shot super-resolution” means transfer of an already learned operator to another resolution; it does not mean no prior training or zero-shot transfer to new combustion chemistry.

**D.** The useful common idea is to specify reusable computation and its representation. The literature does not supply a shortcut from named fuel/material conditions to calibrated extinction, spread or smoke predictions. Benchmark performance in games, language or generic PDEs cannot qualify the NASA families.

## 2. The transferable object is the physical input-to-observable map

**D/P.** A useful abstract decomposition is

\[
\widehat y=\mathcal O_\eta\!\left[
\mathcal P_{\mathcal B,\theta}\big(c,\xi_0,\mathcal G,u_{boundary},g\big)
\right].
\]

`c` denotes declared physical properties/compositions, `xi_0` initial state, `G` geometry, `u_boundary` prescribed forcing, `g` gravity vector, `B` the selected physical reduction/boundaries, and `O_eta` the independently defined observation operator. `theta` denotes only explicitly admitted model numbers. The decomposition is not an implementation or permission to run a solver.

Transfer requires stable meanings for these quantities. A shared gas conductivity must return the same value at the same thermodynamic state in every family. A flux boundary must mean the same transported mass/species/enthalpy, whether it came from a burner feed, liquid evaporation or solid gasification. An optical observation must identify its bandpass/projection/threshold instead of relabelling a reaction contour as a measurement.

The family boundary should reflect phase topology and governing assumptions: a moving liquid interface, a prescribed porous gas feed, a conjugate solid, a jet momentum problem or a population balance. It should not be an investigation identifier disguised as a categorical feature. An apparatus can legitimately contribute its measured geometry, feed convention and detector response. It cannot contribute an outcome-derived correction while being described as conditions alone.

## 3. What must be shared, and what must remain separately specified

| Reusable block | Cross-family obligation | Information not supplied by that block |
|---|---|---|
| Gas thermodynamics/transport | Same species, units, state convention and source version at identical state | Chemical rates, soot yield or surface state |
| Chemical stoichiometry/kinetics | Same reaction/element bookkeeping and mechanism version where actually applicable | Missing low-temperature or heterogeneous pathways |
| Radiation | Consistent spectral properties and energy exchange | Camera contour or detector alarm threshold |
| Interface balances | Conserved mass, elements and energy with declared signs | Liquid activity, pyrolysis or burner thermal boundary |
| Particle mobility/population rules | Consistent drag, diffusion, collision and mass accounting | Unknown particle morphology, charge or source distribution |
| Observation operators | Same instrument semantics and calibration provenance | The underlying state that the instrument cannot uniquely identify |

**D.** Sharing a closure is justified only when its assumptions are shared. Equal-diffusivity mixture fraction may be a common limiting benchmark while multicomponent transport is needed elsewhere. A radiation property can be shared while path lengths and walls differ. A solid's thermal conductivity is not interchangeable with a gas conductivity because both appear in a diffusion equation.

If two families disagree about a genuinely shared property, possible explanations include measurement bias, incorrect state/composition, an invalid closure, missing coupling or an incorrect property source. Introducing a separate constant for each investigation avoids the diagnostic rather than resolving it. A changed shared property requires checking all dependent models; a new source version must not silently alter an immutable release.

## 4. Handwritten origin, external physics and pretrained weights are separate axes

**D/P.** Audit at least the following distinctions:

1. **Author-entered model number:** a literal entry with scientific reason and exact target location in the declared `ModelSpec`.
2. **External physical measurement/correlation:** provenance includes the original physical experiment or published correlation and its domain; manually transcribing it does not make it a first-principles deduction.
3. **Algebraic/geometrical quantity:** derived from declared equations or units. Whether it is a fixed forward operation or a literal model entry must be visible in the frozen specification.
4. **Measured condition/calibration:** independently produced observation with uncertainty and cutoff semantics; it is not a handwritten forecast weight.
5. **External learned network/tensor:** learned parameters retain their training lineage even if downloaded, copied, frozen or serialized as literals.
6. **Outcome-informed revision:** a new construction history with the information/feedback that influenced it, distinct from W0.

The authoring LLM itself is pretrained. A defensible claim is that the particular released numerical model was constructed under the declared no-training/no-feedback protocol. It is not that all information used by the author was unlearned or that public outcome knowledge can be ruled out without a source/exposure boundary.

A precomputed physical-property table can be an externally sourced scientific input if its provenance and allowed construction path are explicit. A neural property surrogate trained externally remains a learned surrogate. Removing its training code or freezing its weights does not turn it into a handwritten property law. Likewise, external mechanism coefficients can have an experimental/fitted origin even when the release was not trained on NASA outcomes; those two claims must not be conflated.

**P.** Registry/construction fields must follow the existing contracts. This note proposes no alternative manifest field or exemption. In particular, the existing requirement for literal `set`/`set_box`/`fill_box` assignments cannot be bypassed with a source citation, generated seed, compiler, parameter-estimation call or numerical file containing generated weights.

## 5. The forward specification carries scientific choices too

**D.** A small patch can hide a large empirical model in preprocessing or the forward. Feature normalization, clipping, interpolation, branch selection, window length and target conversion can all change predictions. Therefore auditing only the edited tensor cells is insufficient.

Before W0 construction, freeze:

- the physical state and equations/reduction represented by the forward;
- input units, fraction bases, initial state and causal cutoff;
- property sources, allowed ranges and behavior for missing/conflicting data;
- geometry/boundary/observation operators and branch admissibility;
- the exact permitted literal patch surface and all fixed numerical constants;
- any approximation or discretization rules and their provenance;
- endpoint definitions and evaluation protocol.

This is a requirement to make the authorized existing mechanism reviewable, not a proposal to insert a new solver. Current prohibitions on solvers, optimization, generated weights and hidden request-time fitting remain in force. A numerical method that is scientifically reasonable is not automatically permitted by the project's WSP experiment.

Input standardization must not use concealed outcome information. Even condition-only means/ranges estimated from an entire archive introduce transductive archive knowledge and should be declared; physically defined unit/scaling conventions are easier to transfer. No scale should be selected by checking which normalization best matches a protected target.

A structurally correct program can still have finite-precision errors, overflow, cancellation, insufficient representational capacity or wrong semantics. A proof about an ideal arithmetic expression is not a proof about every finite network realization. Conversely, storing a large collection of unrelated numbers in a neural architecture does not confer a physical operator structure.

## 6. Concrete transfer claims and their tests

**D/P.** Separate these claims rather than combine them into “generalization”:

| Transfer claim | Prospective evidence needed |
|---|---|
| Investigation-label invariance | Renaming/reordering records with identical physical inputs leaves the output unchanged |
| Species/component permutation | Reordering fully paired compositions/properties leaves physical outputs unchanged |
| Unit/coordinate covariance | Unit conversion and joint rotation of geometry, flow, gravity/electric field preserve physical predictions |
| Shared limiting physics | Family reductions recover the same gas/property/interface limit where their assumptions overlap |
| New apparatus | Safe geometry/boundary/sensor definitions plus independent apparatus-held-out evidence |
| New fuel/material | Required properties and mechanism pathways present, domain justified, and independent evidence or an explicit unqualified scenario |
| Resolution transfer | Declared mathematical/discretization consistency; it does not establish new physical-domain validity |
| New atmosphere/gravity | Retained competing mechanisms and explicit extrapolation limits; no automatic endpoint bound |

Passing the first four checks can establish useful implementation consistency. It cannot establish predictive error on the latter physical transfers. A property's individual range is not the model's joint applicability domain: combinations can cross a chemical branch, introduce a phase transition, change confinement or invalidate an assumed instrument response.

The restricted benchmarks in the family notes deliberately have different mathematical structures: moving-interface inventory, flux-fed spherical mixing, conjugate feedback, advective-diffusive jets, reactive solid history and population moments. Their shared structure is conservation, properties and interfaces. There is no demonstrated universal scalar clock from which every target follows.

## 8. A literal audit that would make the release scientifically inspectable

**P.** Each numerical contribution should be traceable through:

- literal patch location and effective tensor cells affected;
- number origin: external property, mathematical construction, independently measured condition, or author assumption;
- exact source/version, raw unit, conversion and applicability conditions where relevant;
- author rationale and the symbolic role it implements;
- dependency on shared blocks and any earlier candidate;
- exposure/revision state and immutable release identity.

Count literal tokens, distinct values and effective populated cells separately when reporting scale. A box fill can set many cells from one literal; that does not make the remaining numerical structure unauditable or scientifically justified by count alone. Structural zeros and routing constants also participate in the program and should be included in the appropriate construction inventory.

A large table remains large externally supplied knowledge even if typed by an LLM. A measured dataset remains measurements. A learned tensor remains learned. A fixed scientific forward remains an algorithm whose admissibility and approximation error need separate review. None should be hidden under a single “handwritten percentage.”

## 9. Uncertainty and publication limits

**D.** An untrained construction has no automatic empirical calibration of residual error. Property uncertainty, initial-state uncertainty, numerical/representation error, observation uncertainty and missing-physics discrepancy are different contributions. If distributions for uncertain inputs are not justified, report parameter/scenario sets rather than inventing confidence coverage. A probability interval needs a stated probability model and evidence for its interpretation.

The largest scientifically useful claim may initially be narrower than release acceptance: a model can accept typed conditions, conserve the declared quantities and forecast one conditional observable while lacking evidence for another. Report the coverage ladder per investigation and endpoint: metadata available, independent measurement available, target physically representable, runnable accepted release, registered evaluation, qualified domain. Failure at any stage remains visible.

No generalization result from the WSP literature, compiler correctness result, source hash or outcome-free invariant qualifies an IGNIS release. No new investigation is qualified by these notes. A future sealed partial-gravity prediction could be a valuable prospective scientific claim, provided its lack of present validation and its frozen construction are explicit.
